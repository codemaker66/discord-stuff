Set it up

It runs inside Discord in your browser with one helper extension, Tampermonkey: free on Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera and Brave, $2.99 on Safari. You do this once; updates arrive by themselves. Only use the Discord app? That works too.

On your phone, princess? ♡ Discord's phone app can't wear the Purifier~ Open this page on your computer and it takes two minutes ✨

Firefox for Android works too: add Tampermonkey, then open discord.com in “Desktop site” mode.

Your browser:

  1. Add Tampermonkey to Chrome

    Free, made for exactly this. Click Add to Chrome on the page that opens.

    Chrome keeps scripts off until you say yes. Paste this into a new tab, then flip the switch:

    Allow User Scripts

    The panel waits at the top right. Open the chat you want to clear, press Purify, check the count, then hold the red button till it fills. That's it~

Questions

Good to know

Is it safe? Does it send my login anywhere?

It runs entirely inside your own Discord tab (or the app, with BetterDiscord), using the session you're already logged in with — your login and messages never leave Discord.

The userscript talks to Discord and nothing else; Tampermonkey itself fetches new versions of it from GitHub. The BetterDiscord plugin also asks GitHub once each time it starts whether there's a new version. No servers of ours, no tracking~ ♡ The whole thing is open source, so anyone can check.

Can it delete other people's messages?

No. Discord only lets you delete your own messages, and the purifier only ever asks for yours. DMs with anyone you shield are left alone, even while it purifies, and Bardo & Dopa are shielded from the start~ ♡ Shields cover one-to-one DMs, not group chats.

Discord's own notices (calls, people added to a group, renames) can't be deleted by anyone, so those stay too.

Will the other person know?

Discord doesn't notify anyone. Your messages just disappear, the same as if you deleted them one by one yourself, so someone would only notice if they scrolled back and saw your side of the chat is gone. A server's logging bot, or someone running a message-logger mod, may have kept a copy; nothing can reach that.

Can I get deleted messages back?

No — deleted means deleted. That's why Purify counts first, shows you exactly what will go, where and as which account, and makes you hold the button. Pause or Stop any time; nothing after that point is touched.

Could my account get in trouble?

Honest answer: Discord's terms don't allow automating your account, and any tool like this carries a small risk. The purifier works at a human-ish pace (about 40 messages a minute), slows down whenever Discord asks, and never runs on its own — you start it every time. Purify from one place at a time (one tab or the app, not both), so Discord only ever sees that gentle pace. Use it at your own discretion.

How long does it take?

About 40 messages a minute, so 1,000 messages take around 25 minutes — a pace Discord is happy with, and slower whenever it asks for a break. Counting tells you the estimate up front. You can minimise the panel and keep chatting while it works; messages you send after you confirm aren't touched. Keep Discord open until it's done.

Can it find a DM I closed long ago?

Many chats lists every DM open in your sidebar, plus your friends. For anyone else, open the DM in Discord first (search their name, or their profile → Message), then use This chat.

How do updates work?

Tampermonkey checks for new versions automatically. If you want it now: Tampermonkey icon → Utilities → Check for userscript updates. When a newer version is out, this page says so and shows Update now.

On BetterDiscord, the plugin checks each time Discord starts and asks before it updates.

Nothing shows up in Discord — help!

Nine times out of ten it's step 2: on Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave the Allow User Scripts switch (or Developer mode) must be on. Then make sure Tampermonkey is enabled, the script is switched on in its dashboard, and reload Discord. Still stuck? Open an issue.
